we are very happy to announce that tico is officially available for everyone.

## what is tico?

tico is your AI buddy that lives on your screen. it listens to your voice questions, sees what's on your screen, and guides you by pointing to the right spot.

## what's coming next?

- multi-monitor support
- new voices and languages
- integration with more tools
- mac app (coming soon)
