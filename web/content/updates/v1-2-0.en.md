tico v1.2.0 is here with visual improvements, performance gains, and new customization options.

## new look: pointer mode

you can now change tico's appearance in settings. besides the classic blob, there's a new **pointer mode** — a sleek purple triangle that follows your cursor. head to settings and pick the style you prefer.

## microphone selection

if you have multiple microphones connected, you can now choose which one tico should use. the option is right in the settings panel — no need to dig into windows settings.

## faster responses

we optimized the voice recognition pipeline. before, tico waited for local speech recognition to finish before trying cloud transcription. now both run at the same time — whichever finishes first wins. this cuts up to 3 seconds from response time.

## personalized greetings

the first time you open tico, he introduces himself normally. but on subsequent launches, he greets you by name. a small detail that makes a difference.

## installer in english

the installer now detects your system language and shows options in portuguese or english automatically.
